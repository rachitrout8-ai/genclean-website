About GenClean

Reliable backup power, managed more responsibly.

GenClean is a proposed Delhi NCR-based B2B environmental-tech venture. It aims to help organisations understand, maintain and manage their diesel generator systems while exploring cleaner backup-energy alternatives.

Our approach brings together practical maintenance coordination, documentation and sustainability-focused guidance.

Our purpose

Make responsible generator management easier to understand and act on.

✓Mission: Support organisations with organised generator care and informed energy decisions.
✓Vision: Encourage cleaner, more efficient and more responsible backup-power practices.
What we do

Practical services for better generator management.

GenClean's proposed service model focuses on helping organisations keep track of their backup systems and make informed decisions.

🔍

Generator Inspections

Organised visual and operational checklists to help clients understand equipment condition and identify issues for qualified technicians.

🛠️

Preventive Maintenance

Maintenance schedules, service reminders and coordination with qualified generator service professionals.

📋

Digital Reports

Clear service records and inspection summaries that help facility teams track work and plan follow-ups.

🌫️

Emission Monitoring Coordination

Support with documentation and coordination for applicable emissions testing through authorised or qualified providers.

🌱

Cleaner-Energy Consultancy

Initial guidance to help organisations explore suitable alternatives and complementary backup-energy options.

📅

Service Planning

Structured maintenance calendars and follow-up planning to reduce missed checks and improve visibility.
